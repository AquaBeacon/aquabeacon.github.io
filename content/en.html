Underwater positioning.
With light and sound.

AquaBeacon combines optical direction sensing and acoustic ranging to estimate the position of an underwater unit relative to a surface unit. Our goal is to connect underwater imagery with location for environmental surveys.

Open hardware designs, software and research results.

From observations to a position.

The surface unit supplies a GNSS reference and observes the underwater unit's light. Acoustic travel time provides range. Attitude, depth and sensor mounting offsets help relate observations in a common coordinate system.

Current published results are processed from recorded observations. Integrated real-time operation and accuracy against independent ground truth remain to be demonstrated.
